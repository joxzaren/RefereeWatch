# Changelog

Release notes for accepted tickets, newest first.

## REF-3 · Make the watch scheme run the watch app from Xcode

Accepted 2026-11-13.

The watch app can now be installed and run on an Apple Watch straight from Xcode with the RefereeWatchWatch scheme. The README explains how to set up a watch for this, including what to do with a free personal team. The app works the same and still shows as RefereeWatch on the watch.

## REF-2 · Lower the minimum to iOS 26 and watchOS 26

Accepted 2026-11-13.

RefereeWatch now runs on iOS 26 and watchOS 26 or later, so it installs on an iPhone and Apple Watch that are not yet on the newest release. Nothing else changes in how the apps work.

## REF-1 · Apple Watch app with iPhone companion for football referees

Accepted 2026-11-13.

RefereeWatch is a new Apple Watch app for football referees, with an iPhone companion.

On the watch the referee runs the whole match without a phone on the pitch. The match clock keeps exact time with the screen off and shows added time such as 45+2. One tap, a turn of the Digital Crown or the Action button on Apple Watch Ultra starts and stops a stoppage. From five minutes before the end the watch suggests the added time, and the referee can adjust it before announcing it. The watch buzzes 5 minutes and 1 minute before the end, at the end of normal time and when added time runs out. Backup notifications cover the case where the app is put to sleep. Goals, cards and substitutions take three taps, with an optional shirt number, and can be undone or edited. Extra time and penalty shootouts are supported. Each match is saved to Apple Health as a Soccer workout, with the route when location is allowed.

On the iPhone the referee sets up a match before kick off and sends it to the watch, browses past matches, fixes events such as a missing shirt number, and shares a PDF match report. Changes sync both ways between the watch and the iPhone.
